   {{ include "kafka.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}

   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "kafka.fullname" . }} 9092:{{ .Values.service.port }}

   Then use bootstrap server: localhost:9092

3. Single-node KRaft only — not a multi-broker HA setup. For production clusters consider Strimzi or Confluent.
